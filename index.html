About Us

The campaign team.

Meet the people behind the campaign, their responsibilities, and the policy areas they lead.

01 / WEBSITE

Samuel
Cushman

Webmaster / Website Manager

Policy area: National security

Group responsibilities

  • Build and maintain all six website tabs; organize page layouts, navigation, and campaign branding.
  • Publish approved updates to GitHub and manage the GitHub Pages website.
  • Upload the candidate speech, policy positions, citations, campaign images, and commercial when provided.
  • Test links, mobile layouts, image loading, and commercial playback after each update.
  • Research national security topics, including defense readiness, cybersecurity, and foreign threats; draft the campaign’s national security section and document its sources.
02 / COMMUNICATIONS

Hutch
Stennett

Communications Director

Policy area: Social issues and rights

Group responsibilities

  • Coordinate the campaign’s written and spoken messaging so the slogan, speech, and website use a consistent voice.
  • Edit the candidate speech and prepare talking points for presentations and campaign questions.
  • Proofread public-facing copy for clarity, grammar, factual accuracy, and consistency with approved positions.
  • Provide finalized copy to Samuel and coordinate wording for Aiden’s campaign visuals and commercial.
  • Research social issues and rights, including civil liberties, equal treatment, and individual freedoms; draft this policy section and document its sources.
03 / MEDIA

Aiden
Krater

Media Director

Policy area: The economy

Group responsibilities

  • Design campaign posters and other propaganda materials using consistent colors, typography, and the campaign slogan.
  • Plan, storyboard, record, and edit the campaign commercial; coordinate its script with Hutch.
  • Prepare web-ready images for the Store tab and a playable video file for the Media tab.
  • Check audio levels, video readability, and image quality; provide captions or a transcript for the completed commercial.
  • Research economic topics, including jobs, inflation, taxation, and federal spending; draft the economy policy section and document its sources.

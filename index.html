Our Candidate

Aspen Nicol.

Republican candidate for Speaker of the House

Stump Speech

Let me start off by saying that I am not a typical republican candidate, and I will not be held hostage by the typical republican talking points. I will not engage in destructive policies just because my party implemented them, or serve special interests just because they supported my party or my campaign.

Our party is supposed to be the party of freedom. The party that stands up for the rights of its citizens, and shrinks the government. I do not mean to shrink the government just enough that it can fit in our bedrooms as so many liberal advocates have suggested, but rather I want to give you every freedom that is sensible and logical.

I will not restrict abortions within the first trimester. To be clear, I believe abortions are morally wrong, but I do not believe we should give the government unlimited authority over someone's decisions. While in office I will be a very strong advocate for educating both women and men on personal responsibility, and adoption as an alternative. Despite keeping abortions legal, I would still like to see their numbers go down. This is why we will work to support mothers, so that choosing life is a choice they can afford to make.

Now, how will we put that money into the pockets of mothers? I ask this because I don't just want to talk about making things more affordable, I actually want to do it. It starts abroad. Our spending must reflect our principles, and that means cutting all funding to Israel. Something as simple as an alliance cannot require us to abandon our conscience. We must put America above all, but we also must make trade offs. If that trade off means losing an alliance to help shield a people from genocide, that is a trade off I am willing to make.

Here at home, we have to fight corruption, and end the most prominent form of bribery still practiced today. Campaign finance must be reformed so that your vote actually matters. Public office is a public trust, not a return on someone's investment.

Immigration must be addressed. I want less immigration, and I expect more assimilation from those we admit. In the modern day, immigrating to America is a privilege. A privilege that must be earned by learning English, understanding our history, and adopting our culture. Becoming American has to mean more than just living here.

If I leave you with anything, it should be to expect more from your government, more from my party. Expect consistency, expect integrity, and get mad when promises are broken. We can build a government worthy of trust, we just have to put a little more effort into electing the right people.

Make it happen. Vote Aspen.

National security

Position statements by Sam Cushman

Immigration

Immigration: The US should have stronger border security and enforce immigration laws. According to the PEW research center, 70% of republicans said the US-Mexico border is in a crisis, showing that many people believe the government needs to do more to secure the border. PEW also found that 88% of republicans support expanding the border wall, showing strong support for improving border security. These stats support our idea that people should enter the country legally and that stronger border enforcement can help keep the country safe.

Terrorism

We believe that protecting the country from terrorism should be a top priority. According the the PEW research center, 76% of republicans said preventing future terrorist attacks is a top priority, showing that national security is a major concern. PEW also found that 56% of Americans support checking immigrants' social media during the screening process showing that many people support extra security checks. These stats support our idea that stronger security measures and careful screening can help identify and prevent future national threats.

Economy

Position statements by Aiden Krater

Social Security

Social Security should be protected while making sure the program remains financially responsible for future generations. The government should reduce wasteful spending, prevent fraud, and manage taxpayer money carefully to help. Keep Social Security funded benefits for current retirees in people who have paid into the system should be protected. The option to privatize completely should be explored further, and if convincing enough, then implemented in the future. It is also important to make sure the program has enough money to support younger generations in the future. People who have worked hard and paid taxes throughout their lives should be able to receive the benefits that were promised when they retire. The government should look for ways to strengthen Social Security without putting current retirees at risk or unfairly increasing taxes on working Americans.

National debt and government spending

The government should reduce the national debt and control government spending to protect taxpayers and strengthen the American economy. The government should focus on spending money, responsibility, cutting unnecessary expenses, and making sure taxpayer dollars are used effectively. Republicans often support reducing wasteful government programs, lowering taxes, and encouraging businesses to grow so they can create more jobs and help the economy. When the government spends more money than it collects and taxes, it often has to borrow money which increases the national debt and can create financial challenges for future generations. Instead of relying on borrowing, the government should work towards balancing its budget and setting clear spending priorities however, it is also important to protect essential programs such as, Medicare, national defense, and support for veterans
